Why LUNA

Going out at night shouldn't feel unsafe.

The danger is not only on the bus. It is the wait at the stop and the last 500 metres home.

Safety

So you never have to text “home safe” again.

Existing solutions react once danger is there. LUNA prevents it, from the moment you wait to the moment your door closes behind you.

Women drivers

All our drivers are women, trained and employed by a licensed transport operator.

Verified riders

Every rider is verified on her first booking. On board: only women and girls.

Door to door

No more last walk alone in the dark: we drop you at your address, and we wait.

Live trip sharing

Share your trip live with a friend. She knows when you are home.

FAQ

Everything you want to know

Who can ride with LUNA?

All women and girls, whatever their age. Every rider is verified on her first booking.

Why women only?

Because 91% of victims of sexual violence in French public transport are women, and many give up going out at night. LUNA doesn't replace night buses: it picks up where they stop, at the bus stop and on the way home.

How do I book?

Tap “Book”, fill in your pick-up point, time and address: WhatsApp opens with your message ready to send. We confirm your seat.

How can a ride cost only €1?

Schools, employers, venues and local authorities pay most of the cost. The rider pays a symbolic fare, and women on low incomes ride for free.

How far will the minibus take me?

To your door, anywhere in the area served from our hubs at Saint-Charles, Vieux-Port and Cours Julien. La Joliette is coming soon to reach the northern districts.

What about Sundays?

Many of you asked, especially night-shift workers: Sunday nights are coming soon.
